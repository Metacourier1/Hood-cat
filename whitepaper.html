01

Abstract

HoodCat ($HOODCAT) is a community-driven meme project built around a simple idea:

Every hood has a cat. Every cat has a story.

HoodCat isn't trying to pretend that a meme coin is something it isn't. There is no complicated financial narrative, no corporate roadmap disguised as innovation, and no promise of guaranteed returns.

It is a culture-first project built around memes, community, identity and internet-native storytelling.

The mission is simple: build the most recognizable hood cat on-chain.

The Story

Before the charts.

There was just a cat.

But one thing is certain:

HoodCat is outside. And he's not leaving.

The Roadmap

HoodCat doesn't believe in pretending that a roadmap written today can predict everything that will happen tomorrow.

Instead, development happens through phases.

01

Phase 1 — The Arrival

  • Launch $HOODCAT
  • Establish official social channels
  • Launch HoodCat visual identity
  • Build initial community
  • Create memes and lore
  • Establish liquidity
  • Publish contract and token information

Objective: Make people recognize the cat.

02

Phase 2 — The Hood

  • Community meme contests
  • Creator collaborations
  • HoodCat sticker/GIF packs
  • Community spaces
  • Character expansion
  • Viral social campaigns
  • Community-generated lore

Objective: Turn a character into a culture.

03

Phase 3 — Outside The Hood

  • Partnerships and collaborations
  • Community events
  • Merch experiments
  • Additional digital experiences
  • On-chain experiments
  • Creator ecosystem initiatives

Objective: Take HoodCat beyond the original community.

04

Phase 4 — The Legacy

If the community reaches this stage, HoodCat becomes bigger than a token.

The goal becomes building an internet-native character and brand capable of surviving multiple market cycles.

Because memes come and go. Icons stay.
